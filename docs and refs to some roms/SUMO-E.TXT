What's Been Translated
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

I translated everything that used the hiragana font except
for when you get a password wrong. Please, no one tell me
I don't have that screen done! It's encoded somehow, so
I can't translate it! Also, in war mode 07 comes after all
the room names. this is also an encoded thing which cannot
be fixed. I also made a nifty looking silly title screen, I
hope you like it! I left the kanji characters (the big ones)
as they were for three reasons:

1. They would be really annoying to hack.
2. I don't know much kanji.
3. the game just wouldn't be the same without them.

Anyway, despite these little mishaps, most of the game IS in
English and should be enough for any ratioal person to
play with. If any other screwy stuff besides the password
screen comes up, send me a pic of it and I'll see what I
can do about it.
